# Competitive research

Reviewed 13 November 2026. Sources below are official product or pricing material fetched during this task. Prices are dynamic and can vary by geography, billing period, account cohort, included units and additional usage. Published comparisons deliberately compare **billing models**, not a misleading universal starting-price ranking. No quantitative search-volume or keyword-difficulty tools were available.

ORQO evidence: repository product copy in `src/i18n/{en,ar}.ts`, `src/data/{plans,compare,faq}.ts`, existing inbox screenshots, and the live [developer documentation](https://docs.orqo.site) (retrieved with curl; returned contacts, WhatsApp messaging, campaigns and conversations overview). No authenticated app evaluation was performed. These sources support the marketed capabilities; they do not prove every integration or edge case. New content distinguishes verified capabilities from suggested workflows and questions to verify.

## Content distinction

- `/compare/respond-io`, `/compare/wati`, `/compare/manychat`: two-product decision, workflow matrix, suitability in both directions, billing units, FAQs and source dates.
- `/alternatives/respond-io`: five-candidate shortlist, reasons to stay or switch, migration inventory, pilot and cost scorecard. This answers a broader selection question.
- Competitor names identify products only; no affiliation, endorsement or universal “best” claim.
- Sources are visible on each published page. Refresh before publication if the review date is no longer current, and at least monthly for billing claims.
