## Cannibalization rules

- Homepage owns the brand and broad product description. Feature hub is navigation; leaf pages own specific jobs.
- Inbox owns channel consolidation; collaboration owns assignment/handover process; CRM owns profiles/segmentation.
- Product pages answer “what can this do?”; guides answer “how do we organize this process?” Link between them instead of repeating the same introduction and body.
- Solution pages adapt verified capabilities to a particular administrative or commerce workflow. Avoid generic industry substitutions.
- Comparison pages evaluate two products; alternatives evaluate a shortlist and switching decisions.
- Pricing and contact remain the transaction destinations. Use locale-preserving links; do not link Arabic readers to English equivalents by accident.
- Inspect queries/pages in Search Console before adding another page targeting the same family. If intent overlaps, improve the owner or consolidate with a permanent redirect after approval.

## Measurement and iteration

Measure qualified signup clicks, comparison signup clicks and successfully accepted contact forms by landing page/language. Clicks are not completed signups. Use 28-day reporting windows only after sufficient data exists; compare like periods and annotate deployments. Query-level conversion attribution may be unavailable; avoid presenting joins as complete individual-user attribution.
