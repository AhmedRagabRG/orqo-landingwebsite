## Authority materials to prepare

- A concise factual description in Arabic and English, matching the website's verified scope.
- Current screenshots cleared of personal data, with permission to publish.
- A public pricing link, contact details and documentation URL.
- A capability inventory distinguishing available functionality from planned work.
- Case-study permission and review process. Do not add testimonials, ratings, customer logos or quantified results without evidence and permission.

## What to measure

Record listing URL, owner, category, review date, actual referral visits and qualified leads. Evaluate fit rather than buying links for a promised ranking. Do not report domain authority or backlink impact without a reliable dataset. Avoid paid links intended to manipulate search rankings, fabricated reviews and automated community posting.

Review candidate programs again before taking action; the existence of a public vendor page does not establish acceptance or return on investment. Search Console links and referral reports require account access and were not inspected.
