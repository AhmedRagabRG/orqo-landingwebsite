## Keep administrative questions organized
Questions about location, opening hours, available services and how to request an appointment can arrive while reception is handling people in person. A shared inbox helps staff see which enquiries need attention and who will follow up.

ORQO's supported messaging channels, customer context, assignment and AI handover are relevant to these administrative conversations. This page does not establish that ORQO is a medical record system, a clinical decision tool or certified for a particular healthcare regulatory requirement.

## A reception workflow to test
1. A person asks about the clinic's opening hours or location.
2. Reply using information approved by the clinic. An AI agent can assist with these clearly defined administrative questions.
3. If the person wants an appointment, pass the enquiry to the staff member responsible for scheduling.
4. That person checks the clinic's scheduling system, confirms the details and replies.

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking until the scheduling process has established availability. Do not assume calendar synchronization, automatic reminders or electronic medical record integration without a demonstration of the exact setup.

## Define what belongs with a person
Keep diagnosis, treatment recommendations and urgent medical assessment outside an administrative bot's scope. If a message contains a clinical question, staff should follow the clinic's approved process for referring it to an appropriate professional. Do not use a marketing inbox as a substitute for emergency contact procedures.

Limit the information staff request in routine messages. Appointment coordination rarely needs a detailed medical history in a chat. Before using a messaging provider for sensitive patient data, the clinic must assess its own requirements and the provider's contractual and security controls.

## Prepare a useful clinic demo
Bring sample administrative enquiries without real patient details. Test Arabic and English replies if both languages matter, a handover between reception staff and the handling of an unrecognized question. Confirm access controls and data-handling requirements directly with the team. Start with non-clinical information and expand only after the clinic has reviewed the workflow.
