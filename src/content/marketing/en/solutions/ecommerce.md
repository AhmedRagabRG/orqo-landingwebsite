## The communication gap around an order
A shopper may discover a product on Instagram, ask about it on WhatsApp and return later with a delivery question. Different teammates can end up answering different parts of the same story. For a store, the problem is often maintaining context and responsibility between those moments.

ORQO combines supported messaging channels with customer profiles, tags, AI replies and team handover. Use it to organize conversations while keeping your ecommerce system authoritative for stock, payment, fulfilment and refunds.

## A practical product-enquiry workflow
1. Receive the enquiry in the shared inbox and review the available customer context.
2. Answer a routine question from approved product or policy information. An AI agent can help within that defined scope.
3. Assign questions requiring a stock check or exception to a teammate.
4. Verify details in the store system, reply to the customer and record the next step.

If a shopper asks whether an item will arrive before an event, the team should verify the delivery promise rather than let an automated response guess. This example is a suggested process, not a claim of a built-in shipping connection.

## Follow up with relevance
Use customer tags and segments to organize follow-up. A campaign about a relevant collection can be useful to customers who have agreed to receive it. Keep opt-out handling and WhatsApp messaging requirements in the process. A contact's earlier purchase does not, by itself, justify every future marketing message.

Plan team coverage before sending: a good campaign can generate product questions that need timely answers. Review the available campaign report and customer replies rather than assuming a delivery or response metric proves revenue.

## Bring these questions to a demo
Test the actual channels your store uses, the way customer identity is handled and the transition from AI to a person. If you need data from a store platform, ask for a demonstration of that exact integration and the fields it transfers. Do not rely on a generic “integrations” claim to establish order synchronization.

Check account, inbox-agent, automation and AI-credit allowances against your expected workload. Pilot a small part of the process before moving the whole support operation.
