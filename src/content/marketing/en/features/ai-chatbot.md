## Start with questions your business can answer reliably
Delivery policies, opening hours and service descriptions often repeat across conversations. ORQO's AI agent uses business knowledge to reply and can hand conversations over to the team. The quality of the knowledge you provide matters: an outdated policy can produce an outdated answer.

Choose one narrow subject first. Give the agent approved information, identify questions it should escalate and test the resulting conversation in Arabic and English if you serve both audiences. Avoid starting with every possible customer request.

## Keep context through the handover
A useful reply does more than sound fluent. The person receiving the conversation needs to understand what the customer asked and what has already been discussed. ORQO's existing product presentation includes AI replies, conversation summaries, customer context and team handover.

Define who receives escalations and when someone will respond. Test a complaint, an ambiguous request and a question that is missing from the knowledge source. Keep account changes, refunds and other decisions under your team's control unless a specific action has been configured and verified.

## An ecommerce example
A shopper asks how exchanges work. The agent can explain your approved exchange policy. If the shopper describes damaged goods or asks for an exception, a teammate should review the case. This is a suggested workflow; it does not promise an automatic refund or access to an order system.

## Evaluate answers before expanding
Review actual sample conversations with a team member. Look for unsupported promises, language mismatches and missed handovers. Check AI-agent and AI-credit allowances on the pricing page. Expand to another topic only after the initial scope works consistently for your business.
