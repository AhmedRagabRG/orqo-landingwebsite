## Start with relevance and permission
A campaign should answer why this audience needs this message now. An announcement about a new collection may be relevant to customers who requested updates, but not to everyone whose number exists in your records. Review your permission records and the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy before sending.

ORQO's current product presentation includes WhatsApp campaigns, customer segmentation, approved templates and campaign reports. Campaign allowances differ by plan. These capabilities help organize sending; they do not guarantee delivery, engagement or sales.

## Prepare the message and the response path
Define the purpose, select the audience and review the template. Ask a teammate to check the wording, destination links and offer details. Any promise about price, availability or fulfilment should come from the business's current information.

Then decide who will handle replies. A campaign creates conversations as well as outbound messages. Use the shared inbox and customer context to keep those replies connected to the team. Avoid scheduling a send when nobody is available to follow through.

## Review outcomes without making assumptions
Use the campaign's available report to review what happened. Confirm which report fields are provided in your account rather than assuming that an opened conversation proves a sale. Compare the questions customers ask with the message they received and improve the next campaign accordingly.

## Budget for more than a subscription
Check ORQO campaign allowances and any WhatsApp or provider messaging costs that apply to your account. Do not treat a plan's message allowance as a promise that every third-party charge is included. Confirm the complete cost model with the team before a large send.
