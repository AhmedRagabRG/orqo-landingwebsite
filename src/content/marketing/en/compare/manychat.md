## Different starting points, overlapping tools
ORQO presents a customer conversations workspace: shared inbox, customer context, WhatsApp campaigns, AI replies and team follow-up across WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger.

Manychat focuses on social messaging automation for creators and businesses. Its official pricing describes comment and DM workflows, an inbox, AI capabilities and channels that vary by plan. It would be misleading to say that Manychat has no team inbox or AI.

## Compare your actual requirements
| Area | ORQO | Manychat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core workflow | Ongoing conversations and customer follow-up | Social engagement automations and messaging workflows |
| Channels | WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger verified in site content | Connected-channel choices and counts depend on plan; WhatsApp appears in Pro options |
| AI | Business-knowledge agent replies and team handover | AI-powered conversations and Manychat AI in the current Pro offering |
| Customer organization | Profiles, tags, segments and notes | Tags and segmentation documented; test the context your operators need |
| Team access | User and inbox-agent allowances listed separately | User and inbox-seat allowances vary by plan |

## Pricing now depends on active usage
Manychat's current official explanation describes Active Contacts per billing month rather than the older cumulative-contact model. Included contact allowances and overage rules vary by plan and billing period. Check what applies to your region and account, especially if you are on legacy terms.

[ORQO pricing](/pricing) describes its current workspace, connected-account, inbox-agent, AI-credit, campaign and automation allowances. Model a normal month and a busy campaign month in each product. Include channel messaging costs and any extra seats or usage.

## When ORQO may be worth a pilot
Choose an ORQO pilot if your daily priority is following customer enquiries through the inbox and handing work between AI and people. Evaluate the customer context and assignment experience with your actual operators. Do not assume a social comment trigger exists in ORQO without verifying it.

## When Manychat may be worth a pilot
Prioritize Manychat when its documented social triggers and audience-building flows match your acquisition process. Test the exact trigger you need, how a person takes over and whether the selected plan covers your channels and team.

## Test the journey after the first message
For a campaign-driven business, the useful test continues after the automated reply. Have a customer ask an unexpected question, request a person and return later. Compare the operator's context and the cost implications of that activity. Select the product around the complete journey rather than the first impressive automation.
