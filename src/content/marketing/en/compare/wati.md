## Product focus
ORQO organizes WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger conversations with a shared inbox, customer profiles, AI agents and automation. Its plan page separates general user access from inbox-agent allowances; check both when sizing a team.

Wati's official material covers WhatsApp marketing, sales and support, with a team inbox, campaigns, automation and AI tools. It also documents Instagram support. Describing Wati as a WhatsApp-only product would therefore be inaccurate.

## Capabilities to evaluate
| Area | ORQO | Wati |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbox | WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger in a shared workspace | Multichannel inbox listed in current plans; validate your required channels |
| AI | Business knowledge, replies and team handover | Wati AI and Astra agents; limits and tools vary by plan |
| Automation | No-code customer workflows with plan allowances | Chatbot and engagement automation; triggers and integrations vary by tier |
| Customer context | Profiles, tags, segments and internal notes | Tags, follow-up and Commerce/CRM integrations listed by plan |
| Collaboration | Conversation assignment and team handover | Team inbox with routing and team options by plan |

## Understand the commercial model
Wati's pricing page presents subscription tiers, included users, extra-user costs and usage limits, with additional template-message charges. The displayed currency and billing option can vary. [ORQO plans](/pricing) have published allowances for accounts, inbox agents, AI credits, campaigns and automations.

For both products, ask for the cost of the same team and campaign workload, including WhatsApp charges. Do not compare a regional quarterly price with a different product's monthly price as though the terms were identical.

## Who should test ORQO?
Test ORQO if your priority is a shared workflow across the three demonstrated channels and customer context next to the conversation. Bring the team members who will reply, not only the person buying the subscription. Ask them to complete a handover and a follow-up without help.

## Who should test Wati?
Wati is relevant if its documented WhatsApp-oriented tools, Instagram workflows or commerce connections match a concrete requirement. Request the exact plan and integration demonstration. Listed capabilities do not mean every workflow is included at the entry tier.

## A fair test for an online store
Use a product question, a delivery question and a campaign reply. Check how each platform keeps context, assigns responsibility and escalates uncertainty. Keep the store system authoritative for inventory and fulfilment. Choose from the demonstrated workflow, current commercial terms and your team's ability to maintain it.
