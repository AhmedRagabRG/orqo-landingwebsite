## What each product is built around
ORQO combines WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger conversations with customer profiles, AI replies, campaigns and team follow-up. Its public website is available in Arabic and English. For a small team evaluating those channels, these are the core workflows to test.

respond.io positions its platform around managing customer conversations and converting leads across messaging channels. Its official pricing page lists team inboxes, with workflow automation and AI agents on Growth and higher tiers, and advanced integration and security options on higher plans.

## Compare the work, not a checklist score
| Requirement | ORQO | respond.io |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team conversations | Shared inbox, assignment and internal notes | Team and custom inboxes listed in plans |
| AI | Business-knowledge replies and team handover; credit allowances by plan | AI-agent availability and credit rules depend on plan |
| Automation | No-code flows; confirm required triggers and actions | Workflows on Growth and above; advanced actions vary by tier |
| Customer management | Profiles, tags, segments and follow-up context | Contact-oriented workspace; test your needed fields and CRM connections |
| Pricing basis | Published plans with account, agent, AI-credit and automation allowances | Subscription tiers, users and Monthly Active Contacts, with usage rules |

## How to compare total cost
Use [ORQO's current pricing](/pricing) and the official respond.io pricing page. Model the same team size, active contacts, AI workload and WhatsApp sending requirements. Keep billing period, taxes, messaging charges and overages explicit. A starting subscription price alone is not a like-for-like total.

No fixed competitor price is reproduced here: plan selection and usage change the amount. The linked official source explains the current rules.

## When to test ORQO first
ORQO is worth testing if the demonstrated WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger workflow matches your daily work and your team values an Arabic and English product introduction. Verify the actual operator experience and support arrangements in your demo; website localization alone does not prove every product setting is localized.

## When respond.io may fit better
Put respond.io on the shortlist if its documented broader channel and integration options or higher-tier workflow and security features match requirements you have already identified. Verify that the necessary features are included in the quoted tier. This is a suitability assessment, not a claim that ORQO lacks every corresponding capability.

## Run the same pilot in both products
Try a new enquiry, customer lookup, AI answer, human handover and outbound campaign. Test the same examples in both languages. Record setup effort, missed context and the quoted cost for your actual workload. If you're building a broader shortlist, use the [respond.io alternatives guide](/alternatives/respond-io).
